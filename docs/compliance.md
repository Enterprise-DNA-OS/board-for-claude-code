# Governance record checks

Sources checked 4 October 2026. These checks identify evidence gaps in the records here. They do not decide whether an interest is legally disclosable, whether a meeting was valid, or whether an organisation satisfies every duty.

| Rule | Scope and check | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NZ-MINUTE-RECORD | NZ companies: held meetings in the past seven years with no linked minute record. Section 189 requires retention of meeting/resolution records and an interests register. This check cannot detect meetings never entered or validate off-system archives. | [Companies Act 1993, section 189](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1993/105/en/latest/) |
| NZ-INTEREST-DISCLOSURE | NZ companies: recorded known interests with no disclosure date. Section 140 calls for prompt recording and board disclosure, subject to statutory qualifications. The tool uses no invented grace period and does not infer an unlawful transaction. Extent is mandatory text where a monetary value is not recorded. | [Companies Act 1993, section 140](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1993/105/en/latest/) |
| AU-MINUTE-MONTH | Australian companies: held meeting minutes missing after one calendar month, or recorded after that deadline. This uses calendar arithmetic, not a fixed thirty days. A late entry stays flagged after it is recorded. | [ASIC INFO 186](https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/contact-us/reporting-misconduct-to-asic/disputes-about-access-to-company-information) |
| AU-MINUTE-SIGN | Australian companies: recorded minutes without signature metadata need chair review. ASIC says within a reasonable time, so no fixed signature deadline is invented here. | [ASIC INFO 186](https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/contact-us/reporting-misconduct-to-asic/disputes-about-access-to-company-information) |
| POLICY-QUORUM | Every board: held-meeting attendance below its configured quorum. The default of two is a demo setting, not a legal rule. Configure this from the board's constitution. | The organisation's constitution and governance policy |

The configured pack lead time and the fourteen-day quiet-action threshold are operating policies, not statutes. Change them to match the board's practice. The NZ and AU company checks do not apply automatically to incorporated societies, trusts or other entity types. Use jurisdiction other and commission the matching rules before relying on them.

The system preserves records by offering completion and closure rather than delete commands. It does not enforce storage location, retention security, electronic signing, immutable evidence, all shareholder resolutions or every register requirement. Keep original signed documents, confirm access and backup arrangements, and have the secretary review each finding with the relevant professional adviser when interpretation is needed.
